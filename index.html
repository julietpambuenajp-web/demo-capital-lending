About Demo Capital Lending

About Demo Capital Lending

Demo Capital Lending focuses on private real estate financing for investors and qualifying projects. Rather than a one-size-fits-all approach, financing is considered on a project-by-project basis, evaluated around the specifics of the property and the plan behind it.

The goal is straightforward: give real estate investors, builders, and developers a private financing partner who understands investment projects and communicates clearly at every stage of the process.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

What types of real estate projects do you finance?
Demo Capital Lending considers financing for qualifying fix & flip, rental property, bridge, and ground-up construction projects, along with other qualifying real estate investment projects.
What is private real estate lending?
Private real estate lending refers to financing provided outside of traditional bank channels, typically evaluated around the property and the specifics of the investment project rather than a standardized retail lending process.
How does the financing process work?
The process generally follows four stages: submitting your project, review and qualification, discussing available financing options, and moving toward closing once terms are agreed upon and requirements are satisfied.
What information do you need to review a project?
Generally, details about the property, the project plan, and the financing being requested. The lead form on this page collects the core information our team uses to begin a review.
